Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CVPeers ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our CV generation service at cvpeers.com.

Information Collection

Local Storage

Our application uses browser local storage to save your CV data temporarily. This data includes:

  • Personal details (name, email, phone, website)
  • Professional experience
  • Educational background
  • Skills and expertise
  • Languages
  • Summary/Bio
  • Your consent preferences

All data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Technical Information

Our hosting providers (Hetzner and Cloudflare) may automatically collect standard server logs, including:

  • IP addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Access times and dates
  • Referring website addresses

Use of Information

  • Local Storage: Used solely for maintaining your CV data between sessions and remembering your consent preferences until you choose to clear it
  • Server Logs: Used for technical administration, security, and improving website performance

Data Storage and Security

  • CV data and consent preferences are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage
  • Data persists until you clear your browser data or use our "Reset Form" function
  • No CV data is transmitted to our servers
  • Our service is hosted on Hetzner servers and uses Cloudflare for DNS and security services

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your CV data at any time using the "Reset Form" function
  • Clear your browser's local storage to remove all stored data
  • Use the service without providing any personal information

Third-Party Services

Our service uses the following third-party providers:

  • Hetzner: Web hosting provider
  • Cloudflare: DNS and security services

These services may collect technical information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page.
